## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🍕 [KhaiDai — Multi-Role Food Delivery Platform](https://github.com/Anim36/KhaiDai)

A full-stack food delivery platform connecting customers, restaurant partners, riders, and administrators through dedicated portals.

**Key features:** Online ordering, secure payments, live rider tracking, OTP authentication, digital wallet, cashback, referrals, and multilingual support.

**Tech:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, JWT, SSLCommerz, Leaflet.

### 💳 [Loan Reminder](https://github.com/Anim36/Loan_reminder)

A personal loan management application designed to track repayments and automatically send email reminders when payments are due or overdue.

**Key features:** Loan tracking, repayment management, due-date monitoring, and automated email reminders.

**Tech:** Next.js, React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, Auth.js, Nodemailer, Resend.

### 🌾 [AgroSmart BD](https://github.com/Anim36/Agrosmart-bd)

An AI-powered smart agriculture platform designed to support Bangladeshi farmers with digital agricultural services.

**Key features:** AI-assisted crop diagnostics, agricultural information, and smart farming support.

**Tech:** React, TypeScript, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, Google Gemini AI.

### 🤖 [UAP CSE Website — Chatbot Integration](https://cse.uap-bd.edu/credits/)

Integrated an intelligent chatbot into the UAP CSE website to improve user engagement and provide convenient access to information.

**Tech:** Python, Django, HTML, CSS.
